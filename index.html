Bank Run

Every bank in the territory is a stock. Guard the vault, rob it, dig for gold, or hunt the men who did.

WASD or arrows to move · E to use · K to respawn · Switch jobs any time. On a phone: left thumb moves, the brass button acts.

How the Territory Works

Thirty seconds. Then ride.

1 · Move and use

WASD or the left thumb to move. Walk up to a building and press E (or the brass button): the bank takes deposits, the telegraph trades shares, the saloon pours whiskey, the stables rent horses.

2 · The raid

Every 45 seconds the gang dynamites a vault and it stays open for 20. Each sack stolen drops that bank's stock and every depositor's balance by the same percent.

3 · Law and outlaws

Lawmen at the bank when the raid ends earn a share if the vault is still over half full. Outlaws inside the vault press E for a sack: that gold is hot until fenced at the hideout × north-east of town.

4 · The trading day

The closing bell rings every minute. Banks that held pay dividends; if withdrawals pass 40% it's a bank run. Six bells and the day ends: your score is your net worth plus goals met.
